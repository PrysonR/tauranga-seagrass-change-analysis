# Data Documentation

## Study Area
Tauranga Harbour, Bay of Plenty, New Zealand.

## Primary Data Source
**Dataset:** Tauranga Localised Datasets 1943–1960  
**Provider:** New Zealand Department of Conservation (DOC)  
**Original compilation:** Primarily regional council datasets, with contributions from DOC and other agencies.

## Survey Years
- 1943
- 1948
- 1960

## Data Type
Historical seagrass habitat polygons.

## Coordinate Reference System
NZTM2000 (EPSG:2193).

## Data Processing
- Extraction of individual survey years
- Geometry validation and correction
- Area calculations in hectares
- Spatial intersection analysis
- Classification of persistent habitat, mapped loss and expansion

## Important Note
The mapped extents represent historical survey records. Differences in survey methods, mapping accuracy and coverage may influence comparisons between years.
